Work and experience

Three dashboards I built for a superstore dataset, each answering a different business question.

Sales and performance dashboard

Superstore project

The question
How is the store doing overall, and where do sales and profit come from?
What I built
KPI cards for profit, sales, quantity, margin and average discount, a sales map by state, category and segment breakdowns, a monthly trend, and filters for ship mode and region.
What it shows
Technology leads on sales and profit, while Furniture sells well but earns very little. Sales peak in March, September and November.

Power BI · Data visualization · Slicers

Product and shipping analysis

Superstore project

The question
Which products sell most, and how do shipping times and losses affect the business?
What I built
Top products by sales, units sold by sub-category, order counts by shipping delay, a profitability split, a sales versus profit scatter, and ship mode and segment filters.
What it shows
Binders and Paper sell the most units, most orders ship in 4 to 5 days, and about 19% of orders lose money.

Power BI · Data modeling · Interactive filters

Discount and profit by ship mode

Superstore project

The question
Do discounts and shipping choices change how much profit the store makes?
What I built
Average discount by ship mode, profit and quantity side by side, a profit gauge against a 300K target, a profit-by-category ring and summary cards.
What it shows
Standard Class brings in the most profit, First Class gets the biggest discounts, and Technology delivers about half of all profit.
